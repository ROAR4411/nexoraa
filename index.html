    How it runs

    One lead, five steps, zero manual work.

    1. 01

      Capture

      Every inbound signal — form fill, reply, call — lands in Cadence the instant it happens.

    2. 02

      Qualify

      The Inbox Agent scores intent and fit against your criteria and routes it to the right queue.

    3. 03

      Engage

      A first response goes out in your team's voice, on a schedule built from reply-rate data.

    4. 04

      Brief

      Before any human call, your rep gets a two-line brief — history, objections, and a suggested opener.

    5. 05

      Close

      The Forecast Agent rolls the outcome into pipeline the moment a stage changes — no manual entry.
